Version 9 prototype. Firefighter records include service number and FRNSW station selection; station numbers are sourced from the current FRNSW Station Index. Firefighter records now support mobile contact details, optional iOS/browser contact picking where supported, and SMS initiation from PCOS entry. New firefighters can be added directly from Add PCOS and firefighter details can be edited from People. Full 24-hour shift is the primary entry; custom hours is the second option. Balances are calculated from transaction history; settlement creates a normal historical transaction. Data is stored locally in this browser.
